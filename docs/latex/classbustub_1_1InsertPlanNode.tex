\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The \doxylink{classbustub_1_1InsertPlanNode}{Insert\+Plan\+Node} identifies a table into which tuples are inserted.

The values to be inserted are either embedded into the \doxylink{classbustub_1_1InsertPlanNode}{Insert\+Plan\+Node} itself, i.\+e. a "{}raw insert"{}, or will come from the child of the node.

NOTE\+: To simplify the assignment, \doxylink{classbustub_1_1InsertPlanNode}{Insert\+Plan\+Node} has at most one child.
