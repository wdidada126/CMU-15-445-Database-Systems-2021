\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The \doxylink{classbustub_1_1UpdatePlanNode}{Update\+Plan\+Node} identifies a table that should be updated. The tuple(s) to be updated come from the child of the \doxylink{classbustub_1_1UpdateExecutor}{Update\+Executor}.

NOTE\+: To simplify the assignment, \doxylink{classbustub_1_1UpdatePlanNode}{Update\+Plan\+Node} has at most one child.
